Program

Redwood Area School District #2897 provides informational text messages about school closings and delays, attendance, emergencies, events, and other school business. This program is not used for advertising or promotions.

Opt-in

You opt in by submitting the form on the text messages page with the consent box checked, or by texting START or YES to the District SMS number.

Cost and frequency

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to cancel, or use the opt-out form. Reply HELP, use the help form, or call 507-637-0141.

Privacy

See the text messaging privacy statement. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in information are not shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service

Available on most U.S. carriers. Consent to text is not required to enroll or receive school services.
