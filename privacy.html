Text messaging privacy

Opt in Help Opt out Privacy SMS Terms

This statement applies to mobile numbers used for Redwood Area School District #2897 text messages. Student and family data are also protected under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Minnesota Government Data Practices Act.

Information collected

When you opt in on this website or by text, the District collects your mobile number and the name, student name, school, and email you choose to provide. The District also keeps the content of texts sent to and from District numbers and a record of opt-in and opt-out.

Use

The District uses this information to send and receive informational text messages about school closings and delays, attendance, emergencies, events, and other school business, and to honor opt-out requests.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message delivery uses the District’s communications providers.

Choices

Reply STOP to opt out, or use the opt-out form. Reply HELP or call 507-637-0141. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to text is not required to enroll or receive school services.
